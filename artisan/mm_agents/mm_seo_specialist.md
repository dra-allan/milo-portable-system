## TITLE FORMULAS
Pick one. Insert target topic and key number.

- "I [ACTION] [TOPIC] for [TIME] — Here is What Happened"
- "The $[X] Mistake Most [AUDIENCE] Make With [TOPIC]"
- "[TOPIC] Explained: What Nobody Tells You About [SUBTOPIC]"
- "Stop [NEGATIVE ACTION] with [TOPIC]. Do This Instead."
- "[TOPIC] for Beginners: The [TIME] Guide"

Rule: Title must contain a number or dollar amount.

---

## DESCRIPTION STRUCTURE
Line 1 — The Hook: One sentence naming what the viewer loses/gains.
Followed by 3-5 bullet takeaways, each starting with action verb.
Followed by chapter timestamps (6-8 chapters, calculated at 150 WPM).
Followed by disclaimer: "This content is for educational purposes only. Not financial advice."

## TAGS

- Category A — Core Topic (8 tags): personal finance, investing, money management, [specific topic], etc.

- Category C — Channel (5 tags): Money Matrix, financial education channel, YouTube finance, etc.
